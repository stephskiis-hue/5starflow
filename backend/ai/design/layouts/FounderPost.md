# FounderPost

The owners in their own words with a real photo of them. Founder content is the most reliable first winner for a local crew.

Provide: a real photo of Steph and/or Ben, a one-line headline, a short letter under 45 words written with them, and their signature line.

- Do use their real words and real photo; people hire people.
- Don't invent their story, numbers or promises.

Slots: `kicker`, `headline`, `body`, `signature`, `photo` (required).

Slots (for the renderer): `data-slot` text (a line break starts a new line, `[[words]]` sets the accent colour), `data-slot-bg` photo, `data-slot-list` list, `data-if` shown only when that slot has a value, `data-fit` shrinks to fit. Sizes: add `size-story` (1080 × 1920) or `size-square` (1080 × 1080) to `.post`; feed is the default.
