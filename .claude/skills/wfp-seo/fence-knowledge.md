# Fence Pros writing guide

## Voice
A local Winnipeg contractor explaining things to a homeowner across the fence line: plain, specific, practical, honest
about trade-offs. Canadian spelling (colour, metre). Short paragraphs. No hype, no "in today's world", no filler intros.
Never use: leverage, seamlessly, game-changer, cutting-edge, revolutionize, delve, unlock, elevate. No em or en dashes.

## Who reads it
Homeowners in Winnipeg and the surrounding RMs planning a fence, deck, pergola or yard project: what it costs, what the
city allows, which material lasts here, when to build, DIY or hire.

## Local facts to check every time (from a primary source, never from memory)
- City of Winnipeg rules for fences: height limits by yard (front / side / rear), corner-lot sight triangles, pool
  enclosures, whether a permit is needed. Source: City of Winnipeg zoning by-law and its fence / development pages.
- Decks: when a building permit is needed (height above grade, attached to the house), guard and stair rules in the
  Manitoba / National Building Code, footings below frost.
- Utility locates before digging post holes: Click Before You Dig MB (free, required lead time).
- Climate: deep frost and freeze-thaw cycles (frost heave lifts shallow posts), heavy clay soil that swells and
  shrinks, strong prairie winds (fence panels act like sails; pergolas need solid anchoring), snow load on decks and
  pergola tops, short building season (spring thaw to freeze-up).
- Materials as they behave here: pressure-treated and cedar (staining, grey-out), vinyl (cold brittleness, wind),
  composite (heat, expansion), chain link, metal and aluminum posts, concrete vs gravel post setting.
- Property lines and neighbours: surveys, shared fences, the good side facing out.

## Structure that works
1. Two-sentence answer to the search.
2. The detail a homeowner needs to decide: options, costs (typical ranges, sourced, dated), rules, timing.
3. Winnipeg-specific section (climate, soil, city rules).
4. Common mistakes / what to ask a contractor.
5. A natural line to the free estimate.
Titles that work: "Fence Height Rules in Winnipeg (2026): What the City Allows", "Cedar vs Pressure-Treated Fence in
Winnipeg: Cost, Lifespan, Upkeep", "When to Build a Deck in Winnipeg".

## Never
- Quote Winnipeg Fence Pros prices, warranties, years in business, awards or reviews unless the owner's notes give them.
- Present a rule or price without a source you opened this run.
- Write a neighbourhood page with nothing true about that neighbourhood.
